# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that participation in our community will be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful** - Treat all community members with dignity and respect
- **Being inclusive** - Welcome and support people of all backgrounds and identities
- **Being collaborative** - Work together constructively and in good faith
- **Being considerate** - Think about how your words and actions affect others
- **Being empathetic** - Seek to understand different perspectives and experiences
- **Using welcoming language** - Avoid language that excludes or demeans others
- **Accepting constructive criticism** - Listen to feedback with an open mind
- **Focusing on community benefit** - Prioritize what's best for the community
- **Asking for help** - Don't hesitate to reach out when you need support
- **Giving credit** - Acknowledge and appreciate contributions from others

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment** - Offensive comments, intimidation, or unwelcome attention
- **Discrimination** - Treating people unfairly based on protected characteristics
- **Exclusion** - Deliberately excluding people from conversations or activities
- **Bullying** - Repeated hostile behavior or attempts to intimidate
- **Hate speech** - Slurs, derogatory language, or hateful content
- **Sexual harassment** - Unwelcome sexual comments, advances, or requests
- **Threats** - Threats of violence or harm toward any person
- **Doxxing** - Sharing private information without consent
- **Spam** - Excessive self-promotion or off-topic content
- **Trolling** - Deliberately provoking conflict or disruption
- **Disrespect** - Dismissing or belittling others' contributions or experiences

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They will:

- Monitor community spaces and interactions
- Address concerns raised by community members
- Take appropriate corrective action in response to violations
- Maintain confidentiality regarding reporters
- Treat everyone fairly and consistently

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All community spaces (repositories, discussions, issues, pull requests, wikis)
- Official community events and gatherings
- Public interactions where individuals represent the community
- All interactions between community members

This Code of Conduct does **not** apply to:

- Private conversations between individuals
- Content hosted on external platforms or services

## Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience a violation of this Code of Conduct:

### How to Report

1. **Email us** at [conduct@example.com](mailto:conduct@example.com)
2. **Contact a community moderator** directly if you feel comfortable
3. **Use GitHub's reporting tools** for violations on GitHub itself
4. **Reach out to project maintainers** if it involves a specific repository

### What to Include

When reporting, please provide:
- A clear description of the incident
- Any relevant context or background
- Links to relevant comments or messages (if applicable)
- Your contact information (if you're comfortable sharing)

### Confidentiality

We respect the privacy of reporters and will keep reports confidential, except where disclosure is necessary to resolve the issue or comply with legal obligations.

## Investigation & Resolution

Our response process includes:

1. **Acknowledgment** - We will acknowledge receipt of your report
2. **Investigation** - We will investigate the incident thoroughly and impartially
3. **Communication** - We will keep you informed of our progress (as appropriate)
4. **Resolution** - We will take appropriate action based on our findings
5. **Follow-up** - We will check in with affected parties after resolution

## Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:

- **Warning** - A private or public warning
- **Temporary restriction** - Temporary removal from community spaces
- **Permanent removal** - Permanent ban from the community
- **Escalation** - Referral to law enforcement if applicable

The severity of consequences will be proportional to the violation and the individual's history within the community.

## Appeal Process

If you believe a decision was made in error, you may appeal by:

1. Submitting a written appeal to [conduct@example.com](mailto:conduct@example.com)
2. Providing evidence or additional context
3. Waiting for a response from our review team

Appeals will be reviewed by a different team member than the original decision-maker.

## FAQ

**Q: What if I'm unsure if something violates the Code of Conduct?**
A: When in doubt, err on the side of kindness and respect. If you're still unsure, feel free to reach out privately to ask for guidance.

**Q: Can I report anonymously?**
A: Yes, you can report anonymously through email or GitHub's anonymous reporting features. However, providing contact information helps us follow up with you.

**Q: What happens if I accidentally violate the Code of Conduct?**
A: We believe in giving people the opportunity to learn and grow. First violations are often addressed with education and guidance rather than punishment.

**Q: How often is this Code of Conduct reviewed?**
A: We review this Code of Conduct annually and whenever we receive feedback from community members.

**Q: Does this Code of Conduct apply to content outside our community?**
A: Only if it directly affects the safety and well-being of our community members or our community's reputation.
